# Diamond Supply and Value Intelligence Brief

## Executive assessment

The sample market produced **107.9 million carats** in 2024, with an estimated rough value of **$9,533 million**. Production concentration is **moderate** (HHI 0.194). The model's Natural Diamond Value Pressure Index is **12.2/100**.

## Scenario range

The Monte Carlo layer produces a proxy unit-value range of **$77.70–$96.52 per carat** (P10–P90), with a median of **$87.21**. This is a scenario estimate, not a quoted market price.

## Official trade layer

UN Comtrade supplied **172 records** across the configured reporter-year requests. Using each reporter's latest available period, **United Arab Emirates** has the largest combined monitored flow at **$38,187.4 million**. Coverage is **22/24 requested reporter-year combinations**; reporter years differ where the latest period is unavailable, so comparisons are explicitly latest-available rather than falsely synchronized.

Latest periods by reporter: Belgium: 2024, Botswana: 2023, Canada: 2024, India: 2024, Israel: 2024, South Africa: 2024, USA: 2024, United Arab Emirates: 2023.

## Preliminary estimate and forecast

The model estimates 2025 production at **102.8 million carats** (P10–P90: **97.1–108.4 million**). This is a preliminary estimate, not an official KP actual.

The 2026 median forecast is **105.2 million carats**, with a P10–P90 range of **95.2–115.6 million**. Median modeled unit value is **$78.63/ct** (P10–P90: **$66.83–$91.42**). Driver assumptions are transparent and should be recalibrated as producer reports arrive.

## Interpretation

- Higher supply tightness, consumer demand, and trade friction increase modeled value pressure.
- Lab-grown substitution pressure reduces natural-diamond value pressure.
- Carat-level unit values are aggregate customs/production proxies; they do not control for cut, color, clarity, size, shape, or provenance.
- Results support strategic monitoring and research, not investment, sanctions-compliance, or valuation decisions.
